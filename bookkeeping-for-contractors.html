HOW IT WORKS

Less paperwork confusion, clearer business records

A contractor's bookkeeping may need to capture bills that arrive after a job starts, deposits before work is finished, and purchases made across different sites. The best process depends on the business, how transactions are labeled and whether the existing software supports any job-level reporting you want.

Specialized requirements—such as work-in-progress accounting, retainage, certified payroll or complex construction tax treatment—must be discussed separately and aren't automatically included in an ordinary bookkeeping engagement.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about bookkeeping

Do you work with one-person contractors?

Yes. We can discuss routine bookkeeping for sole operators and small crews based on their accounts and transaction volume.

Can you organize records before I meet my tax preparer?

Keeping records categorized and reconciled can make it easier to hand your financial information to your tax professional.

Do you offer certified payroll or advanced construction accounting?

Those specialized services are not promised here. Tell us what your projects require and we can clarify whether the requested work is within scope.
