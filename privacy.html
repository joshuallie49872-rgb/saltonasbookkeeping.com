Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Saltonas Bookkeeping LLC respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we may collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

When you contact us through the website, email, or live chat, you may provide information such as your name, email address, business name, phone number, and details about the services you are interested in. We may also receive basic technical information about how the website is used, such as browser type, device type, referring page, and general traffic information.

How we use your information

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, communicate with prospective or current clients, provide bookkeeping services, improve the website, maintain business records, and protect the security of our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Website service providers

We use third-party services to operate parts of this website. These may include Crisp for live chat, FormSubmit for contact-form delivery, and Cloudflare for website security, performance, and analytics. Those providers may process limited information as necessary to provide their services.

Sensitive information

Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank passwords, full account numbers, tax documents, or other highly sensitive financial information through the public contact form or live chat. If you become a client, we will provide a more appropriate method for exchanging sensitive records when needed.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and business communications for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, maintain business records, meet legal or tax obligations, or resolve disputes. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted or securely disposed of.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information submitted through the website. No website, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have submitted through this website or to request that we update or delete it, subject to any information we are required to keep for legal, tax, or business-record purposes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be sent to saltonasbookkeeping@gmail.com.
