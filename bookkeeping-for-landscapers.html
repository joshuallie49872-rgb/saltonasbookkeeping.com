HOW IT WORKS

A better view of where the money goes

For landscaping operators, costs often arrive long before an entire job is paid. Recording purchases, customer invoices and deposits consistently makes it easier to review the financial picture. The right system depends on your size, workflow and what reports your current software can produce.

If you already have an accountant or tax preparer, organized bookkeeping records can simplify that relationship. We can discuss whether job-level tracking is practical for your needs; specialized payroll, tax filing and advanced construction accounting are not automatically included.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about bookkeeping

Can you help with a small one- or two-person landscaping business?

Yes. We can discuss an appropriate scope for a small operator and the transaction volume involved.

Can you help with last season's bookkeeping?

We can assess the statements, payment records and bookkeeping data available for the months that need catching up.

Will you track materials by individual job?

That depends on your software, how purchases are labeled and the service scope. Tell us what you need to see by job so we can discuss feasibility.
