HOW IT WORKS

What to expect when you get started

We start by learning which accounts and software you use, how many transactions you typically process, and how current your records are. From there, we can discuss a scope of work appropriate for the business. If your books are several months behind, we can assess a separate catch-up project before ongoing monthly services begin.

Clean, current records can also make it easier to discuss your business with a lender, a tax preparer or a business partner. Bookkeeping is not the same as an audit or tax return preparation; we'll be clear about the services included in your agreement.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about bookkeeping

Can you help if I'm behind on bookkeeping?

Yes. We can review the months involved, the statements you have and the condition of your existing records before deciding what cleanup is needed.

Do I need to have QuickBooks already?

Not necessarily. Tell us which bookkeeping and payment tools you use so we can discuss a workable process.

Do you serve businesses outside Portland?

Yes. Saltonas Bookkeeping is based in Portland, Oregon, and offers remote-friendly bookkeeping support.
