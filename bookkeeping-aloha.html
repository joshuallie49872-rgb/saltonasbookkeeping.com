BOOKKEEPING FOR YOUR WORKFLOW

Practical records for the way your business operates

For a sole proprietor, the most useful early improvement is often not a complicated accounting system; it's a consistent distinction between business activity, owner payments, processor fees and ordinary operating costs. A simple review each month can prevent a larger cleanup later.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Aloha bookkeeping questions

Can you work with a solo business owner?

Yes. We discuss service scope based on the number of accounts and transactions rather than requiring a large company.

What if I paid a business expense personally?

Keep the receipt and describe the transaction so it can be reviewed and recorded appropriately within the bookkeeping scope.

Is there a physical Saltonas Bookkeeping office in Aloha?

We don't advertise a separate Aloha office. Saltonas Bookkeeping is Portland-based and offers remote-friendly service.
