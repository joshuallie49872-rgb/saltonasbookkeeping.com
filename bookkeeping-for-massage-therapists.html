HOW IT WORKS

Why a bank deposit doesn't always match a sale

Payment processing fees, refunds and timing differences can make booking-platform sales reports look different from what reaches your bank. A consistent reconciliation process helps explain those differences and avoids treating every deposit as though it were the original gross sale.

For ordinary bookkeeping, the focus is financial data rather than private treatment notes or clinical client information. We can discuss which summaries and statements are actually needed for the bookkeeping task.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions about bookkeeping

No treatment notes are requested for routine bookkeeping. We focus on the business financial records needed to reconcile transactions.

What if I use a booking platform rather than QuickBooks?

Tell us which software you use and what financial reports it provides. We can discuss the most suitable bookkeeping process.

Can you help a sole proprietor working from one treatment room?

Yes. We can discuss a scope suited to your business size, accounts and payment methods.
